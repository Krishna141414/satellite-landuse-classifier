How it works

  • Data: EuroSAT RGB (Helber et al., 2019) – 27,000 64×64 Sentinel-2 tiles, 10 classes. Stratified 70/15/15 train/val/test split.
  • Model: MobileNetV3-Small pretrained on ImageNet, last layer replaced by a 10-class head (~1.5M params).
  • Training: first only the head (backbone frozen), then all layers with a 10× lower learning rate. AdamW, random flips, early stopping on validation loss.
  • Demo: model exported to ONNX and run with onnxruntime-web. Your image is resized to 224×224 and normalised in the browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Limitations

  • Trained only on European Sentinel-2 RGB tiles (~10 m/pixel). Photos from other sensors, zoom levels or regions may be misclassified.
  • Always picks one of 10 classes – it cannot say “none of these” (e.g. a city photo or a cat).
  • Uses RGB only; the 13-band multispectral data would separate crops and vegetation better.
  • Similar-looking classes cause most errors: Annual Crop vs. Permanent Crop / Pasture, and Highway vs. River (thin linear features).
  • Test images come from the same dataset, so real-world accuracy is likely lower.
Dataset: Helber et al., “EuroSAT”, IEEE JSTARS 2019 · Built with PyTorch & onnxruntime-web
